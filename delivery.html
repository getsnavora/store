Delivery options

Deliver your way

Two ways to get orders to customers. Pick the one that fits how your store works today.

Navora Delivery

Navora assigns an available Navora rider to collect the packed order from your store and deliver it to the customer.

Your own riders

Connect your store riders in seconds

Riders join your store with a short code — no paperwork at the counter.

  1. Store adds riderAdd the rider from Riders in the Navora Store app.
  2. Code generatedThe Store app shows a temporary four-digit store connection code.
  3. Rider enters codeThe rider types the code while setting up the Navora Rider app.
  4. ConnectedAfter verification, the rider is linked to your store and can receive your orders.

Not a delivery OTP. The store connection code only links a rider to your store. It is separate from customer delivery OTPs and other order verification codes.
