Confirmed Cash on Delivery

Cash orders are tracked too

With Confirmed COD, the customer pays a small Order Confirmation Fee online and the rest when the order arrives. Both parts of the order are recorded, so cash orders show up in your settlements like any other order.

Each completed order is recorded as a settlement in the Store app, where you can follow settlement status and payout history.
