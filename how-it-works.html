How Navora works for your store

Customers order from nearby stores on Navora. Your store prepares the order, a rider delivers it, and everyone can follow its progress.

How Navora Store works

From customer order to doorstep

See the full process

    Customer orders

    A nearby customer orders from your store on Navora.

  2. 02

    Store receives order

    The order arrives in your Store app with an alert.

    Store accepts

  4. 04

    Products packed

    Rider collects

    A Navora rider or your own rider picks it up.

  6. 06

    Customer receives

    Delivered to their location, tracked live.

At your counter

What your team does at each stage

  1. Get the alertA new order appears in the Navora Store app with its items, total and payment type.
  2. Accept or rejectAccept if you can fulfil it. Reject if you can’t — the customer is told.
  3. Prepare and packPick each item, pack the order and mark it ready for pickup.
  4. Hand over to the riderA Navora rider or your own rider collects the packed order.
  5. Settlement recordedOnce delivered, the order is recorded in your settlements.

Delivery options

Deliver your way

Two ways to get orders to customers. Pick the one that fits how your store works today.

Navora Delivery

Navora assigns an available Navora rider to collect the packed order from your store and deliver it to the customer.

Your store
Navora rider
Customer
  1. Store prepares the order
  2. Navora rider is assigned and collects it
  3. Rider delivers to the customer

Suits stores without delivery staff.

Your Own Riders

Already have delivery staff? Add them to Navora, connect them through the Navora Rider app, and assign orders directly.

Your store
Connected rider
Customer
  1. Add your rider in the Store app
  2. Rider connects with the Navora Rider app
  3. You assign orders; they deliver

Suits stores that already deliver.
